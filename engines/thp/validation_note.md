## Root cause
The textbook reads z from the Standing-Katz chart manually.
Hall-Yarborough (1974), our correlation, is an analytical fit to the same chart
but produces z values ~0.7-0.8% higher in the Ppr 1.4–1.5 range.

## Impact on THP
- Engine THP: ~149 psia
- Textbook THP: ~110 psia
- Difference: ~39 psia (~35% relative)
- This difference is entirely due to z-factor accumulation over 170+ steps

## Project implication
This does NOT affect the research validity. The ML surrogate model will be
trained on data generated by this engine (HY-based), and validated against
this same engine. Internal consistency is what matters for surrogate modeling.
The z-factor correlation choice is documented and cited.
